# Product Analytics Associate

We are looking for an early-career Product Analytics Associate to support product and business teams with reliable reporting, KPI tracking, experimentation support, and data-quality monitoring.

## Responsibilities

- Write SQL to pull and analyze product and customer data.
- Maintain recurring reports and dashboards.
- Track KPIs including activation, engagement, retention, and operational performance.
- Support experiment measurement and summarize results.
- Build and maintain reliable analytics datasets.
- Monitor data quality and investigate anomalies.
- Communicate findings to cross-functional stakeholders.

## Qualifications

- 1–3 years of analytics experience, including internships.
- Strong SQL skills.
- Python experience preferred.
- Experience with Tableau, Looker, or similar BI tools.
- Familiarity with cloud data warehouses preferred.
- Strong analytical thinking and communication skills.
